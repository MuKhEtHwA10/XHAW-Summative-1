1.  Introduction
This report presents a design proposal for the development of a web page and mobile application for a small- to mid-size enterprise (SME) operating within the gaming and esports industry. The purpose of the proposed digital platform is to advertise the business, allow potential customers to submit booking requests, and provide automated quotations for gaming experiences and events. The report outlines the scenario selected for this project, the motivation behind the choice, the client's requirements, the principles of user-centred design that inform the proposed solution, and the visual identity elements — namely the logo and colour palette — that have been developed to reflect the client's brand.
2.  Selected Scenario
2.1  Scenario Chosen
The scenario selected for this project is Scenario 3: Gaming & Esports Arena, an SME initiative that requires a web page and a companion mobile application to advertise the business, manage booking requests, and generate quotations for a range of gaming experiences and events.
2.2  Description
The business operating under this scenario is Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena, established in Johannesburg in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer who identified a gap in access to high-performance gaming equipment and organised competitive events in the local market. The arena has grown into a modern entertainment venue that caters for casual players, competitive gamers, schools and corporate clients alike. It hosts gaming events, esports tournaments, birthday parties, school outings and corporate team-building events.
The venue offers two categories of bookable products. The first category, Gaming Packages, includes the Ultimate Gamer Pass, VIP Gaming Experience, Esports Training Package and Birthday Party Package, each priced at R1 500. The second category, Individual Experiences, includes the Virtual Reality Experience, Racing Simulator Challenge and Escape Room Challenge, each priced at R750. Customers — including individuals, families, schools, gaming clubs and businesses — may select more than one booking at a time, and a tiered discount structure rewards multiple bookings: two bookings receive a 5% discount, three bookings receive a 10% discount, and more than three bookings receive a 15% discount. This pricing and discount structure has direct implications for the required booking and quotation functionality of the proposed platform, as the system must be able to calculate combined bookings and apply the correct discount automatically.
3.  Motivation for Choosing the Scenario
This scenario was selected because it offers a well-defined yet suitably complex design brief that draws on multiple aspects of user-centred digital design. The business caters for a diverse user base — from individual gamers and families to schools and corporate clients — which creates a meaningful design challenge in balancing a playful, high-energy brand identity with clear, functional booking and quotation workflows. The tiered discount structure, in particular, provides an opportunity to demonstrate how logical business rules can be translated into an intuitive digital experience. In addition, the client's explicit request for a modern, high-tech visual identity with vibrant colours offers considerable scope for creative exploration in logo and colour palette design, making the scenario well suited to demonstrating both design reasoning and technical execution.
4.  Client Requirements
4.1  Who is the Client?
The client is Jason Naidoo, the founder of Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena. Jason is an avid gamer who established the business to create a venue where gamers of all ages could access the latest gaming technology, compete in esports tournaments, and socialise with friends. His venue caters for a broad user base, including casual players, competitive gamers, schools and corporate clients, positioning the business as a community-oriented gaming and events destination rather than a niche esports facility alone.
4.2  What Does the Client Want?
Jason requires a web page and mobile application that will:
●	Advertise Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena and its range of Gaming Packages and Individual Experiences.
●	Allow potential customers to submit booking requests for one or more packages or experiences.
●	Automatically generate quotations that reflect the tiered discount structure based on the number of bookings selected.
●	Reflect a modern, high-tech gaming environment through the selected images, fonts and colours.
●	Communicate vibrant, exciting visuals that appeal to a wide audience, from casual gamers and families to schools and corporate clients.
These requirements form the foundation for the user-centred design decisions and the visual identity developed later in this report.
7.  User-Centred Design
7.1  Definition
User-centred design (UCD) is an iterative design approach in which the needs, goals, behaviours and limitations of end users are placed at the centre of every stage of the design and development process (Norman, 2013). Rather than designing a product around technical constraints or business assumptions alone, UCD requires designers to understand who the users are, observe how they intend to interact with a system, and test and refine design decisions against real user feedback. The International Organisation for Standardization formalises this approach in ISO 9241-210, which defines human-centred design as an approach to systems design and development that focuses specifically on making systems usable by involving users throughout the design lifecycle (International Organization for Standardization, 2019).
7.2  Importance
User-centred design is important because it directly affects whether a digital product is usable, accessible and ultimately successful. For Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena, the user base spans casual gamers, competitive esports players, parents booking birthday parties, schools organising outings, and corporate clients arranging team-building events. Each of these user groups approaches the platform with different goals, technical familiarity and expectations, which means the booking and quotation process must be simple enough for a first-time visitor while still efficiently supporting a returning customer who books regularly. Applying UCD principles ensures that the navigation, booking flow and quotation calculator are intuitive and error-free, reducing the likelihood that a potential customer abandons a booking due to confusion or frustration (Krug, 2014). In addition, a user-centred approach improves customer trust and satisfaction, as an interface that anticipates user needs and communicates clearly is more likely to convert visitors into paying customers and repeat bookings — both key business objectives for Jason's venue.
8.  Logo Design
8.1  Logo Explanation
The proposed logo for Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena is built around a combination mark that pairs a geometric emblem with the business wordmark. The emblem stylises a gaming controller silhouette merged with an upward-pointing chevron, referencing the phrase 'Next Level' while doubling as a subtle play button and progress indicator — reinforcing ideas of advancement, competition and achievement that resonate with both casual and competitive gamers. A thin circuit-line motif traces along the edge of the emblem to reinforce the high-tech, esports-driven identity the client requested. The wordmark uses a bold, geometric sans-serif typeface with squared-off terminals, chosen for its strong legibility at small sizes (important for mobile app icons and social media avatars) and its association with technology and gaming interfaces. A secondary, lighter-weight version of the same typeface is used for taglines and supporting text to maintain hierarchy without introducing a second typeface family. The overall mark is designed to work as a full horizontal lock-up on the website header and as a standalone emblem for the mobile app icon and favicon, ensuring visual consistency across every customer touchpoint.
9.  Colour Palette
9.1  Colour Palette Explanation
The colour palette was developed to fulfil the client's requirement for a modern, high-tech gaming environment with vibrant colours and exciting visuals, while remaining functional across a website and mobile application interface. A dark charcoal base was selected as the dominant background colour, as dark interfaces are strongly associated with gaming platforms and esports broadcasts, reduce eye strain during extended use, and make accent colours appear more vivid by contrast (Eiseman, 2017). Electric purple and cyan were selected as primary accent colours to create a sense of energy and technology; both colours are widely used within the esports and streaming industry and provide strong contrast against the dark background for buttons, links and key call-to-action elements such as the 'Book Now' button. A neon green was added as a secondary accent for success states, confirmations and highlighted promotions, as green is conventionally associated with 'go' actions and positive feedback in interface design (Interaction Design Foundation, 2023). Finally, an off-white was retained for body text and form fields to preserve readability and accessibility contrast ratios in line with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (World Wide Web Consortium, 2023).

10.  Conclusion
This report has outlined a design proposal for Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena's web page and mobile application, grounded in the client's requirement for a platform that advertises the business, manages booking requests, and generates quotations that reflect its tiered discount structure. By applying user-centred design principles, the proposed platform is intended to serve the arena's diverse customer base — from casual gamers and families to schools and corporate clients — with an intuitive and accessible booking experience. The proposed logo and colour palette translate the client's brand vision of a modern, high-tech, vibrant gaming environment into a cohesive visual identity that can be applied consistently across the website, mobile application and supporting marketing materials. Together, these design decisions provide a strong foundation for the next stages of the project: wireframing, prototyping and development.
